On the menu, ← → picks a game, ↑ ↓ sets the speed, Space sets the starting level, and Enter starts.

While playing, arrows move, Space rotates or fires, Enter pauses, M toggles sound and R resets. Hold ↑ in Racing or hold your direction in Snake to go faster. In Filler, each shot adds a brick to plug a gap, and full rows clear.
